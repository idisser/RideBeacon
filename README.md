# 🚴 RideBeacon — Investor One‑Pager  
Anonymous, real‑time proximity networking for cyclists  




---

## **Overview**
RideBeacon is the first **anonymous, real‑time discovery network for cyclists**, enabling riders to find compatible nearby cyclists *during* a ride — not before or after. Using a low‑energy Bluetooth beacon, RideBeacon broadcasts minimal, non‑identifying ride data and allows optional post‑ride connection only with mutual approval.

---

## **The Problem**
Cyclists who want to ride together face three persistent barriers:
- No way to know who nearby is open to forming a group  
- Approaching strangers mid‑ride is awkward and unsafe  
- Existing apps (Strava, Garmin, Meetup) only connect riders **before** or **after** rides  

Result: missed social opportunities, fragmented communities, and less safe solo riding.

---

## **The Solution**
RideBeacon creates a **real‑time proximity layer** for cycling.

During the ride:
- Anonymous Ride ID  
- Speed band  
- Direction vector  
- Ride type (social, training, exploring)

After the ride:
- Riders review encounters  
- Mutual opt‑in unlocks optional contact sharing  

Privacy is built in from the ground up.

---

## **Key Features**
- **Ride Radar** — live view of nearby riders  
- **Group Formation Mode** — link up with compatible riders  
- **Post‑Ride Review** — see who you encountered  
- **Mutual Opt‑In Sharing** — no identity exposure without approval  
- **Safety Broadcast** — emergency ping to nearby riders  
- **Ride History** — optional log of recurring encounters

## **Competitive Landscape**
No existing platform offers anonymous, real‑time discovery:
- Strava → social + analytics, not proximity  
- Garmin → hardware ecosystem, no anonymous networking  
- Meetup/Eventbrite → pre‑planned rides only  
- WhatsApp/FB Groups → manual coordination  

RideBeacon is the **only** proximity‑based cycling network.
